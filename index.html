How the numbers work

Attendance comes from the box score of each final game. Schools report it, and most report tickets distributed rather than people in the seats, so treat it as the official figure, not a headcount.

Rankings and the D1 average count only home games at a team's own venue with a reported figure. Neutral-site events and games against non-D1 opponents still appear in the game list. Games with no figure show as "not reported" and are left out of every average.

Capacity is each arena's listed basketball seating, from Wikipedia's list of NCAA Division I basketball arenas, matched to the venue in the box score. Some crowds top 100% because schools sell standing room or report tickets distributed. Venues with no confirmed capacity, mostly neutral-site arenas, show a dash and are left out of % of capacity.

For context, the NCAA's 2024-25 final count put D1 home attendance at 4,373 per game.
